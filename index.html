About me

I am an international student from Bangladesh, now studying Computer Science in the United States. I like turning everyday problems into simple web tools: a daily sales tracker for small shops, a call planner for different time zones, and games for study breaks.

Before college I worked in sales and customer service, led a team as captain in an international English-language competition, and took part in national math and biology olympiads. Those experiences taught me to listen, explain clearly, and keep learning.
